ABOUT US

Welcome to Los Pollos Hermanos, where quality, tradition, and great taste come together! Founded by Gustavo Fring, Los Pollos Hermanos is a famous fast-food restaurant known for its delicious fried chicken and friendly service. Our restaurant is built on the belief that good food brings families and communities together.We take pride in serving delicious meals prepared with carefully selected ingredients and special recipes. From crispy fried chicken to tasty burgers and satisfying sides, every meal is designed to give our customers a wonderful dining experience.Our restaurant represents quality, cleanliness, teamwork, and hospitality. We believe every customer deserves a warm welcome and excellent service. Whether you are visiting with family, friends, or colleagues, Los Pollos Hermanos is the perfect place to enjoy a delicious meal.
